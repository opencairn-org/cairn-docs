# Gear

Gear is the bikes and shoes you ride and run in. Since 0.4.0 it is a real
entity: you manage it on the **Gear** page, your providers' gear is mapped onto
it, activities get their gear automatically, and every piece of gear shows its
distance, moving time and activity count.

## Your gear is the golden record

Cairn keeps **one record per physical bike or pair of shoes**, yours. A
provider's record of the same bike (Strava's "Canyon", Garmin's "Grail") is a
*provider record* mapped onto it: one gear can carry several provider records,
one per connected account. That is what makes "bike A on Strava is bike B on
Garmin" a single bike in Cairn.

Provider records are never turned into gear on their own. When a connected
account reports gear Cairn has not seen, the record stays **pending** and you
get a *Gear needs a decision* notification. On the Gear page you choose:

- **Create gear** — new gear from the provider's description (name, brand,
  model, retired state, defaults).
- **Map** — the record is an alias of gear you already have.
- **Decide later** — nothing changes; the record stays pending.

A mapping can be corrected later by mapping the record onto other gear.
Nothing a provider sends ever overwrites a name, brand or model you edited; a
retirement on the provider side is followed.

## How an activity gets its gear

- **Imported activities** take the gear recorded by the winning source. Each
  source carries its provider's gear id; Cairn resolves it through the
  provider records to your gear and applies your merge policy's priority for
  the field group `gear`. Like any field group it can be pinned to one source.
  A source whose record is still pending does not count until you decide.
- **Manual and uploaded activities** get your **default per sport**, once,
  when they are created. Changing a default later does not rewrite history.
- **Your choice wins.** Pick gear on the activity edit page (including *no
  gear*) and it stays, whatever later imports say. The activity page shows
  whether gear was *set by you* or came *from source*; **Reset to automatic**
  hands it back to the merge.

## The Gear page

- Active gear with totals (distance, moving time, activity count; a click
  lists those activities), provider badges, and the sports it is the default
  for.
- Create, edit, **retire** (keeps history, hides it from pickers), bring back,
  and **delete** (its activities keep no gear, and are re-derived from their
  sources).
- **This is the same as…** merges two records: provider records, defaults and
  activities move to the one you keep. Not reversible.
- **Defaults per sport** for manual and uploaded activities.
- Pending provider records with the three choices above.

Totals count every live activity attached to the gear, using the activity's
corrected distance and moving time where you overrode them.

## Syncing provider gear

Gear lists are fetched from each account on connect, with every scheduled
reconcile, and on **Sync gear** on the Connections page. Workers that do not
import gear say so there.

Activities imported before 0.4.0: Strava sources carry the gear in their
archived data, so a **reparse** (Manage → sources) attaches it without API
calls; Garmin sources need a **reimport**.
